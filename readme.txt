Global and account-level model explainability is implemented for the selected churn prediction model.
Primary and additional churn drivers are identified for individual accounts.
Churn risk bands are generated based on predicted churn probabilities and agreed thresholds.
Churn drivers are presented in a business-understandable format.
Model results, churn drivers and risk bands are reviewed with the Business SME.
Business feedback is documented and agreed changes are incorporated.
