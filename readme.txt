You can ask the business very simply:

> “FR-RR-001 defines the rolling 12-month observation period and the snapshot date. For the churn model, can you confirm what future period after the snapshot should be used to determine whether the member actually churned?”



Then give an example:

> “For example, if the snapshot date is 31 Dec 2024, should we assess churn over the next 3 months, such as 1 Jan to 31 Mar 2025, or should a different outcome period be used?”



Even shorter:

> “Can you confirm the required churn outcome window after each snapshot date? We currently use the previous 12 months for features, but need business confirmation on how far forward to measure actual churn.”
